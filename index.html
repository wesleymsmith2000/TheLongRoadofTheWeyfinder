Help

Controls

Keyboard: WASD/arrows move, mouse aims, Space fires secondary, Q/E cycles secondary, Tab cycles guided targets, Esc/P pauses.

Controller: left stick moves, right stick aims, bumpers cycle secondary, L2/R2 cycle guided targets, Start pauses.

When a menu is open, left stick/D-pad moves the cursor, A/B clicks, and right stick scrolls or changes selects.

Mobile: left side drags movement, right side aims and fires.

Right stick moves an aim reticle. Comp aim bends manual aim against vehicle velocity so shots pass through the selected point.

Combat

Primary fire can use gunner AI. Secondary weapons trade ammo, heat, cooldown, and impact style.

Destroyed enemies stop blocking shots, explode, and scatter nearby enemies away from the firing lane.

Scrap

Surviving enemy voxels become scrap. Gold pieces magnetize toward the craft at about 30 voxel range.

Between levels, spend scrap to repair systems, replace detached modules, and replenish selected secondary ammo.
