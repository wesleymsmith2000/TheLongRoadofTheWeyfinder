Drive the machine. Lose the machine. Keep the Core moving.

Help

Controls

Keyboard: WASD/arrows move, mouse aims, click fires secondary, Q/E cycles secondary, G toggles gunner AI.

Controller: left stick moves, right stick aims, triggers rotate, bumpers cycle secondary.

Mobile: left side drags movement, right side aims and fires.

Combat

Primary fire can use gunner AI. Secondary weapons trade ammo, heat, cooldown, and impact style.

Destroyed enemies stop blocking shots, explode, and scatter nearby enemies away from the firing lane.

Scrap

Surviving enemy voxels become scrap. Gold pieces magnetize toward the craft at about 30 voxel range.

Between levels, spend scrap to repair systems, replace detached modules, and replenish selected secondary ammo.
