# Weyfinder Road Car Enemy Content Handoff

Editor/content checkpoint target: `v1.0.8.35`

This handoff records the level-1 Weyfinder Road enemy rebuild from boat silhouettes into car-style sculpted cell constructs. It also adds the level-3 side-strafing flechette racer.

## New Construct Assets

Files:

- `content/examples/prototype0-zone-enemy-set/constructs/example.construct.weyfinder_road_car_sculpted.json`
- `content/examples/prototype0-zone-enemy-set/constructs/example.construct.weyfinder_road_armored_car_sculpted.json`
- `content/examples/prototype0-zone-enemy-set/constructs/example.construct.weyfinder_road_flechette_racer_sculpted.json`

All three constructs are generated by:

- `scripts/generate-sculpted-zone-enemy-constructs.mjs`

Shared anatomy:

- exactly one `core` cell
- four 2x2 `wheel` blocks, for 16 `wheelBlock` cells total
- one armor-cell shell around each wheel block
- eight `wheelEngineMount` `engine` cells where the wheel assemblies meet the body
- explicit structural connections with `connections.length === cells.length - 1`

Fast lookup tags:

- `dev-lookup:weyfinder-road-car`
- `dev-lookup:weyfinder-road-armored-car`
- `dev-lookup:weyfinder-road-flechette-racer`

## Level Wiring

Runtime selection in `src/core/game.js` now routes:

- `TheWeyfindersRoad_1` and `TheWeyfindersRoad_2` standard spawns to `weyfinder_road_car.prototype0`
- `TheWeyfindersRoad_3` standard spawns to a mix of `weyfinder_road_car.prototype0` and `weyfinder_road_flechette_racer.prototype0`
- future enhanced Weyfinder Road spawns to `weyfinder_road_armored_car.prototype0`
- `BossFight_1` and future `TheWeyfindersRoad_*Boss*` tracks to `boss.weyfinder_road_hotrod.prototype0`

The old `level <= 6` boat fallback was removed from `usesBoatSilhouetteEnemy`, so Weyfinder Road no longer falls back to pirate hulls.

## Zone 1 Hotrod Boss

Runtime factory:

- `createRoadBossCarEnemy`

Runtime anatomy:

- oversized rounded car hull
- four scaled-up rounded wheel pods
- four-cell connected core cluster
- `roadBossBladeLauncher` gun cells for enemy blade barrages
- `roadBossBulletGun` cells for standard bullet barrages

Behavior:

- alternates between orbiting the player and side-to-side U-turn strafe runs
- fires `road-boss-blade` projectiles using the player blade-launcher shard sprite at `1.5x` scale
- fires `road-boss-bullet` standard bullet barrages between blade volleys
- uses the shared car spinout behavior after more than two wheel groups are destroyed
- uses internal boss destruction
- near the final explosion, emits a tongue-out taunt and launches five `escapePodBoat` pirate skiffs toward the ocean/offscreen
- escape boats do not fire, do not drop scrap, and show taunt cues while sailing away

## Flechette Racer Behavior

New pattern:

- `content/patterns/enemy_tracking_flechette_strafe.json`
- asset id `enemy_tracking_flechette_strafe`

The live runtime uses the existing `carBehavior` hook from `docs/race-car-enemy-runtime-handoff.md`:

- `kind` / `movement`: `raceStrafe`
- side-entry spawn from outside the road bounds
- tracking flechettes that drift, then accelerate toward the player
- spinout after more than two wheel blocks are destroyed
