# Arcade runtime rules

The title screen's **Arcade Run** starts `runMode: 'arcade'` with a fixed vehicle, editable loadout and **Guided AI** targeting. Both the simulation and targeting selector use that default for new runs; saved targeting preferences still restore. `createGame` and weapon-bay normalization accept legacy `'lite'` input and normalize it immediately. The starter asset is `arcade_starting_vehicle`; its JSON filename remains `lite_starting_vehicle.json`.

Arcade overrides live in simulation code. Normal and Sandbox retain their authored weapon balance and movement. No platform APIs are needed to test Arcade.

- The body faces the current road frame's local negative Y axis, including during road turns. Bodies are authored facing local negative Y, so `vehicle.heading = road.heading`; projectile angles still use `arcadeForwardHeading()` (measured from positive X). The visible primary barrel and muzzles share that firing axis. Translation stays available; rotation input cannot change facing.
- Ordinary primary launches aim along road-forward, independently of cursor, turret and compensated aim. Main Gun retains its seeded random angle offset on both sides of that axis, reduced by accuracy upgrades. Mortar keeps its selected/guided target and exact-target arc. Secondary Beam and the Vortex Wavelet Beam launch forward; the wavelet locks that forward path when triggered. Cannon instead aims directly at its firing-time reticle snapshot and detonates at that location, with enough lifetime to reach distant targets. Its fuse origin and target both follow road scrolling. Impacts can still detonate it earlier, and shots without a reticle retain their ordinary expiry fuse. Utility beams retain threat-directed aiming.
- Mini Beam is an Arcade utility, installed in `utilitySlots.slots` as `mini_beam`. It fires automatically from a functioning utility cell when a live, unphased enemy center is within its upgraded beam length, even with primary autofire off. It uses its normal damage, heat and cooldown, and its existing upgrade family. Normal mode retains Mini Beam as a primary.
- Each gun cell has one primary and one secondary slot in Arcade. Only explicitly installed weapons in active slots on functioning, attached cells can fire; empty cells never generate a basic gun. Secondary fire originates at a cell holding that specific weapon.
- Three gun cells start active: two with both slots available, the third with only its secondary available. Each zone boss clear opens one more slot: the third cell's primary first, then primary followed by secondary on each remaining cell. Unlocks cost no scrap and survive save/load. Fully inactive gun cells have a grey X in the launch editor and Workshop, and on the vehicle during upgrade/repair stops. Hovering explains the boss-clear requirement.
- The Arcade starter keeps its 114-cell outline and smoothed shoulders, with single-cell systems: one Core, two engines, four wheels, two utilities and eight separate gun hardpoints. Surrounding armor replaces repeated system tiles. There is no player cell enlargement pass. Enemy module enlargement remains unchanged, as do Normal vehicles.
- Main Gun bullet lifetime is 4.4 seconds, twice the Normal 2.2 seconds, preserving its projectile speed and seeded spray.
- Player handling scales the same hull's acceleration and braking by 2.25 and its base maximum speed by 4, after normal module/damage and upgrade calculations. These compound both handling increases: another 50% acceleration/braking and doubled top speed over v1.0.17.0. Boost and terrain effects continue to apply. Normal handling is unchanged.
- Arcade road travel starts at 60 world units/second, twice the Normal rate. Terrain tile edges grow from 32 to 128 world units; chunk edges grow from 512 to 1024, so each chunk has 8x8 tiles instead of 16x16. Doubling chunk distance alongside travel speed preserves base chunk turnover. `cachePixelSize: 512` keeps cached images at their previous pixel dimensions; rendering paints 64 tile entries per cache instead of 256. This is a measured work-count reduction, not a browser frame-rate guarantee. Explicit Sandbox runs use default terrain and road speed.
- If no enemies are visible for 15 seconds, the next normal wave is brought forward into view, including distance-triggered waves. With no normal wave pending, a required Diver wave enters directly at its top tracking band; distant surviving Divers instead allow a normal reinforcement. The watchdog pauses with gameplay, and resets on a visible enemy or level transition. Authored Sandbox schedules are exempt.
- Two seconds without visible scrap or scheduled enemies spawns one themed gap Diver directly in its tracking band. Only one gap Diver is alive at a time. It can fight and drop scrap, but never pauses scheduled Diver timers, counts toward required waves, blocks completion, or resets the 15-second scheduled-wave watchdog. A separate seeded RNG supplies its movement/fire randomness. A new normal or Diver wave sends it straight offscreen without further firing; leaving the view removes it without a death explosion or reward. Victory removes any remaining gap Diver.
- The Arcade progress bar combines stage progress (50%) with required Diver progress (50%). Each required wave contributes a quarter of the bar, including partial progress from destroyed members of its active wave. The HUD also shows the cleared-wave count out of two. Gap Divers are excluded, and only completed levels reach 100%.
- Tracking Flechettes launch forward, retain the delay and turn phase, and acquire the active enemy nearest to the moving player. Their steering ignores reticle and projectile proximity.
- Ordinary primary projectile launch velocity is axial. Backward and lateral player motion cannot slow or deflect launch. For base speed `v0` and nonnegative player speed `vp` along the firing axis, world speed is `vp + v0 * (0.5 + 0.5 * exp(-vp / v0))`. This is a spawn-only rule; subsequent homing, forces and ricochets remain free to alter velocity. Mortar's exact-target arc is exempt.
- Primary cooldowns double after upgrades. All projectile impact, beam and blast damage gets another 50% increase over v1.0.17.0, including utility beams, secondaries and both teams. This compounds the earlier offensive-primary and enemy damage doubling to 3 times Normal damage; secondaries and utilities use 1.5 times Normal. Derived fragments inherit scaled damage once; authored emission/burst payloads scale at their own spawn. Ram and environmental damage remain unchanged.
- Ordinary bullets from both teams (including Diver guns and boss nodules) and player flechettes/shrapnel have 1.5 times their previous collision radius, sprite dimensions and travel speed. Flechette acceleration and speed caps also scale by 1.5 so delayed/homing launches retain that increase. Enemy flechettes, blades, missiles and mortar arcs retain their sizes and speeds. Their damage still receives the common increase.
- Enemy projectile timers advance at half rate, including pattern emitters, boss arms, artillery, mines, missiles and special beams. Frog hop movement stays unchanged and fires on alternating hops.
- All explosive radii grow by 50% after upgrades, for both teams. Derived explosion visuals reuse the already scaled radius. Deflected shots retain their existing scaling.
- Blade Launcher launches at half speed with 125% collision radius and sprite size. Deflection upgrades start from a 33% base. Its 50% seeded ricochet roll is separate from retained damage (`ricochetFactor`), and only successful bounces count toward `maxRicochets`. A failed roll preserves trajectory and lifetime.
- Ordinary enemies recover toward a band 55?85% of the distance to the visible road-forward edge, with a preferred center at 72%. The player movement lane is smaller than the visible view and does not define enemy staging. Numeric `road.viewWidth`/`viewHeight` come from viewport configuration, including mobile zoom; headless simulations fall back to `road.halfHeight`. Recovery leaves lateral movement intact. Charges, dives, first side/behind entries, boss sweeps, stun, escape, anchored obstacles and linked body segments retain movement ownership. Diver cycles own their movement entirely.
- Between specified maneuvers, bosses recover toward a farther-forward band (66-90%, centered on 80%) and track a reachable lateral position near the player's lane. Once inside, their centers stay within the movement lane with a footprint margin. Scripted strafes, dives, flyovers, entry crossings, retreat, spinout and phasing retain movement control; returning bosses steer back into bounds before containment resumes.

Arcade Diver waves, themed variants, hopper predation and creator JSON fields are described in [Arcade Divers](arcade-divers.md). Automatic waves are Arcade-only, with at least two cleared waves required before a level completes.

The Arcade Vortex Wavelet Beam is an explicit-trigger ultimate attack. All tiers use twice the base charging attraction, 1.5 times the authored length and 75% of the authored capture/pulse widths. The source vehicle stays pinned relative to road scrolling throughout charge, including against steering, boost and collision impulses. Source-gun destruction cancels the pin; discharge releases it with a 140-unit/second backward velocity kick. Scene displacement uses 2.25 times the tier's authored visual strength (25% less than v1.0.18.0), with no straight charging guides. A soft teal fog-light glow builds from 0.5% to 33% peak opacity along the axis. Deterministic teal particles begin just beyond the capture boundary and accelerate inward, increasing in density as charge builds. The renderer snapshots the scene once per active frame and uses a bounded 32-by-6 displacement mesh without pixel readback.

The charge pulls live enemy projectiles as well as movable enemies toward the firing axis. The final shock returns hostile projectiles outward and forward at three times their speed before attraction (or their current speed if first encountered at discharge), transfers them to the player team, and disables their old homing/delayed thrust and targeting fuse. Exactly axial shots return straight up the firing axis. The release uses shared geometry for the bright front, expanding optical rings and distorted ripple envelope; collision sweeps those bands across frame steps. Every live enemy voxel covered by that visible area takes damage once, including enemies outside the old narrow central strip. Phase-protected pirate cores and phased-out enemies retain their protection. Damage per covered voxel is at least a full level-10 Particle Beam burst: level-10 damage and firing duration, summed with the same per-frame beam envelope, with the common 50% damage increase. The existing authored wavelet damage is retained as a floor. Normal/Sandbox wavelet tuning remains unchanged. See [wavelet runtime fields](weapon-bay-wavelet-runtime.md).

The weapon/utility manifest is available in all run modes: always in pause, and as a lower-left overlay toggled by GEAR or the rebindable M shortcut. Browser storage remembers overlay visibility. Every installed active slot is shown separately, including duplicate weapons and systems whose mounting cells were destroyed or detached. Artwork and names use 50% opacity. Teal means healthy; yellow means local integrity below 50% or structural support below 70%; a red X means detached, destroyed, missing or at most 10% system integrity. Structural risk follows the strongest remaining explicit connection path to the core, accounting for weak supporting cells and anchor grip. Empty/locked Arcade slots are excluded; repaired and replaced systems update automatically.

Creator-facing `projectile.ricochetProbability` is optional, defaults to `1`, and accepts values from `0` to `1`. The same field is supported on emission and detonation payloads. See [creator extension fields](creator-extension-api.md).

Older Arcade multi-slot loadouts retain slot zero; overflow weapons move into Workshop inventory, and legacy primary Mini Beams move to free utility slots or inventory. Run mode is recorded in exported saves. Normal/Sandbox equipment balance is unchanged.

`tests/arcadeMode.test.js` covers normalization, curved-road facing, primary direction/pacing, the launch-spacing floor, Blade statistics, Flechette targeting, both teams' explosions, special enemy timer paths, seeded ricochets and front recovery. `tests/arcadeLoadout.test.js` covers boss progression, installed-only firing, utility targeting, forward secondaries, legacy migration and empty-screen wave timing. Run `npm test` and `npm run build` for the complete checks.
